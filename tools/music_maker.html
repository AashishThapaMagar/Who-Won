WHO WON? MUSIC MAKER

Nepali instruments in your browser. Draw a beat and a tune, then export a WAV for the game.

Drums madal, dhime, jhyali — click squares; each column is one eighth note, 4 bars

Bansuri flute — one note per column; the same note in squares side by side is held long

Sarangi bowed — notes next to each other slide into one another (meend)

Tips: start from a preset and change a few squares. Keep the loop calm under fights; leave the drums sparse in the first bars. Space plays/stops. Export WAV renders your loop the number of times set by Repeat, with the reverb tail, and saves it. Send the WAV (or the saved pattern) to Claude to put it into the game for any arena.
